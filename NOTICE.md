## Data attribution

### WFP Nigeria Food Prices

The core price data is the **WFP Nigeria Food Prices** dataset, distributed by the
**Humanitarian Data Exchange (HDX)**.

- Publisher: World Food Programme (WFP)
- Distribution: HDX
- Licence: **CC BY-IGO** (Creative Commons Attribution for Intergovernmental Organisations)
- Use: recorded market prices for tomato and onion in Nigeria

Tomionce is an independent product. It is not endorsed by, affiliated with, or sponsored by WFP
or HDX. Any errors in interpretation or presentation are Tomionce's own.

### Demonstration data

The two pilot markets labelled **Demonstration data** use synthetic observations. They are
generated for product demonstration and are never presented as real market records.

## No financial, trading or investment advice

Tomionce provides price intelligence. It does not buy, sell, broker, transport or store produce.
It does not provide financial, investment, legal or trading advice. A recorded price is not a
guarantee of the price a user will be offered. A forecast is a range with evidence attached, not
a promise.

Users should check the observation date, unit and source before acting on any number.
